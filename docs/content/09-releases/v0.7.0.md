- **Resized images.** With `images.resize: true`, PNG and JPEG images get smaller copies (by default 480, 800 and 1600 pixels wide) listed in a `srcset`, so phones and small screens download a fraction of a large screenshot or photo. Copies are cached between builds, so only new or changed images are resized.
- **Photos taken on their side** get the right `width` and `height`, and their copies show the right way up.

See [Resized copies](../02-writing/03-links.md#resized-copies) and the [`images`](../03-configuration/01-config-file.md#images) config section. To keep the cache between CI builds, see [Deploying](../06-deploying.md#resized-images).

Upgrading: nothing to change. Resizing is off unless you set `images.resize: true`. If your theme's content column is not about 46rem wide, also set `images.sizes`.
