description: Math fixes for narrow screens and systems without a default math font.
---

- **Math on narrow screens.** A wide `$$...$$` formula inside a paragraph scrolls in its own box instead of widening the page, and wide formulas can be scrolled with the keyboard. Printed formulas no longer show a scrollbar.
- **Math fonts.** The built-in theme asks for common math fonts by name (Cambria Math, STIX Two Math, Latin Modern Math, Noto Sans Math), so brackets stretch and limits sit right where the browser's default would not.

Upgrading: nothing to change.
